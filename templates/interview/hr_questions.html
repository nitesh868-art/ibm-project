Golden Rules for HR Interviews
📋 Preparation
  • Research the company thoroughly
  • Review your resume — know every line
  • Prepare 2-3 stories using STAR method
  • Prepare 5 questions to ask the interviewer
🎯 During Interview
  • Be specific — avoid vague answers
  • Use numbers when possible ("reduced by 30%")
  • Listen carefully — don't interrupt
  • It's OK to pause and think
⚠️ Common Mistakes
  • Badmouthing previous employers
  • Being dishonest about skills
  • Not asking any questions at the end
  • Memorised, robotic-sounding answers
