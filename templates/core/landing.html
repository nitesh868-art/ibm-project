The Problem

Students Are Unprepared for Placements

Most B.Tech students face placement season without a clear preparation strategy. The result? Missed opportunities at dream companies.

😞

No Clear Study Plan

Students don't know what to study, in what order, or for how long — wasting precious preparation time.

😟

Unknown Skill Gaps

Without analysis, students don't know which skills they are missing for their target companies.

😖

Resume Not ATS-Optimized

Most resumes get rejected before a human even reads them because they fail ATS screening.

😵

Interview Anxiety

Without structured practice, students freeze during real interviews despite knowing the material.

The Solution

One Platform for Complete Placement Preparation

PlacementPro uses NVIDIA Nemotron AI via OpenRouter to analyze your profile and provide personalized, actionable guidance — not generic advice.

  • AI analyzes your skills and identifies exact gaps
  • Personalized study plans based on your schedule
  • ATS resume scoring with improvement suggestions
  • AI mock interviews with real-time evaluation
  • Transparent placement readiness score
  • Daily AI recommendations based on your progress
