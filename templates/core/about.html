About PlacementPro

Built by B.Tech students, for B.Tech students. A comprehensive AI-powered portal to prepare for campus placements.

Our Mission

PlacementPro was created as a B.Tech Major Project with a vision to democratize placement preparation. We believe every student deserves access to quality preparation resources, regardless of their background.

Our AI-powered platform combines the best of technology — OpenRouter & NVIDIA Nemotron AI, Django backend, and modern UI — to create a comprehensive learning management system specifically designed for engineering students.
